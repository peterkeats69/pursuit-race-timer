Race officer notes

GitHub edition: Install from your browser where supported. Use Backup settings before race day, and test voice and hoots with your actual device. Full-screen availability depends on the browser; installed app mode gives a cleaner display.

Arm the race before the first start and use Test voice to check your tablet's volume. Only the first fleet has the five-minute class flag, four-minute P flag, one-minute P flag down and start signal. Each first-fleet flag movement has warnings at 60, 30 and 20 seconds and a spoken 10-to-1 countdown. Subsequent fleets receive start warnings at 60, 30 and 20 seconds and a spoken 10-to-1 countdown, with a short hoot at the start. Short electronic hoots mark movements; P flag down has a long hoot. Finish warnings are at 30, 20, 10 and 5 minutes; each minute thereafter; 30 and 20 seconds; then 10 to 1 and FINISH.

Times are derived from the device's local clock, not accumulated timer ticks. If you change the schedule, save it and re-arm to reset warnings. Keep the page visible and the device awake: browsers may throttle or suspend speech and timers when locked or in the background. Use a separate official timepiece and horn as backup. Spoken alerts are aids, not guaranteed precise signals.
